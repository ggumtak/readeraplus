Some fonts map characters to glyphs that have no outline (for example Hangul syllables outside KS X 1001). So
that the device's own font draws those characters instead of a blank, the app may keep, in its private cache on
the device, a copy of such a font file whose character map leaves those characters out. That copy is made on the
device, used only to display text in this app, and never distributed; the files shipped with the app are unmodified.
